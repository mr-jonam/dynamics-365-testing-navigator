# Dynamics 365 Testing Navigator: decision guide

Start with the application before selecting the testing conversation.

**For:** Dynamics 365 programme owners, business process owners and QA leads who need to clarify application boundaries.

**Decision in focus:** Which application family and business journey determine the evidence discussion?

## Clarify what Dynamics 365 means in this change

Dynamics 365 names a family of applications. Begin with the application involved and the business outcome changing. A Finance and Operations process, a CRM journey and a connected Power Apps experience may raise different evidence questions.

**Question to discuss:** Which application and business outcome are actually in scope?

## Read tool references within their application scope

Microsoft documents RSAT for Finance and Operations apps. Microsoft documents Test Studio for canvas apps. These references do not establish a universal testing tool choice for all Dynamics 365 applications, model-driven apps or connected services.

**Question to discuss:** Does the application type match the scope described by the proposed tool documentation?

## Look beyond one application screen

Data movement, business roles and cross-application handovers may affect acceptance. Consider them only where relevant to the proposed change. The public conversation should use category labels, not tenant identifiers, records, environment exports or access details.

**Question to discuss:** Who owns evidence when the business process moves into another application?

## Agree the question before requesting a solution

A concise discovery brief states the application family, change category, process outcome and unresolved acceptance question. It does not need implementation exports. The delivery approach should follow a private, authorised discussion of the actual landscape.

**Question to discuss:** Which evidence question is preventing a clear business decision?
